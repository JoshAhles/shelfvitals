Questions
Does this replace my sync app?

No — it audits it. ShelfVitals never writes to your store or your marketplaces. It reads both sides, compares them, and tells you when they disagree. It works alongside Marketplace Connect, CedCommerce, LitCommerce, Trunk, or any setup — including manual syncing.

How do oversells happen if I already have a sync app?

Silently: a missed webhook, an expired token, a listing edit that breaks the link, a sync queue that stalls. The marketplace keeps selling against stale numbers, and the sync app's dashboard still says connected. Reporting its own failure indicts its own product — which is why the audit has to be independent.

Can ShelfVitals change anything in my store?

No. It's architecturally read-only — no write permissions requested anywhere, and no buyer personal data stored, ever.

Which marketplaces at launch?

Faire and Amazon first (including Amazon suppressed-listing detection), then Etsy, eBay, Walmart, and TikTok Shop. Early-access members vote on the order.
