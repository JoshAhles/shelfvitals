Amazon suppressed your listing — and didn't tell you

Suppressed listings are the quietest revenue leak on Amazon: the ASIN drops out of search or goes inactive, sales flatline to zero, and Amazon doesn't reliably notify you. Multichannel sellers feel it worst — with orders still flowing from Shopify and other channels, one channel going dark blends into the daily noise.

Where suppressions actually show up

  1. Seller Central → Inventory → Manage All Inventory — check the status column for Inactive.
  2. The "Fix Your Products" view — search-suppressed and at-risk listings live here, often without any notification ever sent. If you've never opened it, open it today; most sellers find something they didn't know was dark.
  3. Account Health — policy-driven deactivations (pricing errors, compliance documents, restricted keywords).

Common causes for Shopify multichannel sellers

  • Feed/connector overwrites. Your sync app pushes a title, image, or attribute that violates a category style rule (image background, title length, prohibited terms) — Amazon suppresses rather than rejects, and the connector reports success.
  • Missing required attributes after a category schema change — Amazon periodically adds required fields, and existing listings get suppressed until they're filled.
  • Pricing-error suppression — a Shopify-driven price update trips Amazon's fair-pricing checks (often a currency or per-unit mistake introduced by the connector).
  • Expired compliance documents on regulated categories.
  • Variation family breaks — a connector re-pushing parentage can orphan child ASINs; orphans go inactive.

Fix path

  1. In Fix Your Products, every suppressed ASIN names its specific issue — fix the attribute, image, or price it names, resubmit, and status usually recovers in hours.
  2. If the cause was your connector's feed, fix it at the source (Shopify data or connector mapping) — otherwise the suppression boomerangs on the next sync.
  3. For policy deactivations, Account Health has the appeal path — respond with exactly the document or correction it names, factually and fast.
The real cost is detection time. A suppressed ASIN found same-day is a two-hour fix. Found after three weeks, it's three weeks of zero sales, dead ranking momentum, and stale inventory decisions layered on top.
