Shopify & Etsy inventory not syncing? Diagnose it in 15 minutes

The nasty thing about a broken Shopify↔Etsy sync is that nothing announces it. The connector app still says "connected," Etsy keeps selling, and you find out from an order you can't fulfill. Here's a fast way to establish what's actually broken, the usual causes, and how to stop being the last to know.

Step 1: Spot-check five SKUs by hand

  1. Pick five products you sell on both channels — include your two best sellers (they drift most, because they change most).
  2. Open each in Shopify admin (actual available quantity) and on Etsy (listing quantity). Write both numbers down.
  3. Any mismatch on current stock = live drift. Etsy higher than Shopify = oversell risk right now. Etsy lower = you're silently underselling.

Step 2: Check the order pipeline, not just inventory

Inventory drift and order-import failure are different breaks with different fixes. In Etsy: Shop Manager → Orders. In Shopify: Orders (filtered to your Etsy integration's channel or tag). Every recent Etsy order should exist in Shopify within minutes. Missing orders mean the import leg is down — which also silently breaks any fulfillment automation (Printify and similar) hanging off Shopify.

The usual causes, in order of likelihood

  • The connector silently lost auth. Etsy API tokens and permissions expire or get invalidated (password changes, Etsy API policy updates). Most connectors keep showing "connected" from cached state. Fix: disconnect and reconnect the integration with the shop-owner account.
  • Etsy API changes broke the app. Etsy has repeatedly tightened its API, and older connectors degrade partially — inventory keeps syncing while orders stop, or vice versa. Check the app's changelog, then its recent reviews: if the last 30 days of reviews describe your exact problem, it's them, not you.
  • SKU/variant mapping broke. Renamed variants, edited SKUs, or relisted items orphan the link between a Shopify variant and an Etsy listing. Orphans stop updating but stay live on Etsy — the classic source of "it sold on Etsy but Shopify said 0."
  • Location mismatches. Multi-location Shopify inventory (or a connector syncing only one location) sends Etsy a number that's real but wrong.
  • Sync queue stalled. Some connectors batch updates; a stalled queue means everything works — hours late. At best-seller speed that's enough to oversell.
Rule of thumb: the damage isn't the break, it's the time to discovery. A sync that broke this morning costs an apology. A sync that broke two weeks ago costs refunds, Etsy customer-service stats, and review damage.

How to catch the next one early

Once fixed, the reliable habit is a daily reconcile: export Shopify quantities for your shared SKUs, compare against Etsy's, and eyeball recent Etsy orders against Shopify. Fifteen minutes a day, forever — or automate exactly that comparison.
